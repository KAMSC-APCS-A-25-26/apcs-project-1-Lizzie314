You are an elf in the world of Enchanted. You have to make various decisions throughout the game to try to collect the
crystals and gems that give the people in your world power. In one game mode, you are going on an adventure and search
on a mountain to collect crystals, where you will encounter villains trying to get the crystals from you. In the other
game mode, you are going on a quest around the city to collect gems that have been hidden. Throughout the game, you
make major decisions that can be risky. One wrong move and you fail at your mission.
